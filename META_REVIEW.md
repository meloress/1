## 1. App description (umumiy)

> UzBot is a Telegram bot (@uzchatgptaibot) used by small businesses in Uzbekistan. A
> business owner connects their Instagram Professional account through Instagram Login
> inside the bot. After that, an AI assistant answers customers' Instagram Direct messages
> on the owner's behalf, using the business information (prices, address, opening hours)
> and the reply style the owner configured in Telegram. The owner chooses the mode:
> "Assistant" mode sends every proposed reply to the owner in Telegram for approval;
> "Auto" mode replies directly and pauses automatically in a chat as soon as the owner
> writes there themselves. The owner can disconnect at any time (/biznes → Instagram →
> Disconnect), which deletes the access token immediately.

## 2. instagram_business_basic

> We use instagram_business_basic to read the connected account's user ID and username
> right after the owner logs in (GET /me?fields=user_id,username), to identify which
> owner an incoming webhook belongs to, and to read a customer's public name and username
> (GET /{IGSID}?fields=name,username) so the owner sees who wrote in their Telegram
> notifications. We also read the caption of the owner's post when a customer comments on
> it, so the reply refers to the right product. No data is used for advertising or shared
> with third parties.

## 3. instagram_business_manage_messages

> We use instagram_business_manage_messages to receive Instagram Direct messages sent to
> the connected business account (messages webhook) and to send replies on the business's
> behalf (POST /me/messages). Replies are generated by an AI assistant from information the
> business owner provided, and in "Assistant" mode every reply is approved by the owner in
> Telegram before it is sent. We also set ice breakers (FAQ buttons shown when a customer
> opens the chat) via /me/messenger_profile, and show typing indicators and read receipts.
> Photos and voice messages a customer sends are converted to text only to understand the
> request. Conversation history is kept for at most 90 days and deleted after the owner
> disconnects.

## 4. instagram_business_manage_comments

> When a customer comments a question on the business's post (for example "price?" or
> "+"), we use instagram_business_manage_comments to receive the comment (comments
> webhook), send one private reply to that commenter in Direct (POST /me/messages with
> recipient.comment_id), and post a short public reply "📩 Direct'ga yozdik" ("We replied
> in Direct") under the comment (POST /{comment-id}/replies). Comments that are not
> questions (emojis, compliments) and the owner's own comments are ignored. We never
> delete, hide or edit customer comments.

## 5. Human Agent

> The Human Agent tag is used only for messages written or explicitly approved by the
> business owner (a person): a draft reply the owner approved with a button in Telegram,
> or a message the owner asked the bot to send to a specific customer. It is applied only
> when the standard 24-hour window has closed, so that the owner can still answer a
> customer's question within 7 days. Automatic AI replies never use this tag — the code
> sends them without a tag and they fail outside the 24-hour window.
